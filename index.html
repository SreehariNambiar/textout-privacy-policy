6. Events, Hangouts & Memories

Events and Hangouts are temporary groups with a set start and end time. Posts shared within an Event or Hangout are visible only to its participants and are automatically deleted when the Event or Hangout ends — unless a user chooses to save specific content as a "Memory" before it expires. Content saved as a Memory is retained the same way as any other content you post, and remains subject to this Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions.

Because other participants may also save shared content as their own Memory, deleting your account does not automatically remove copies that other users have separately saved before the Event or Hangout ended.
